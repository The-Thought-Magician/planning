The Physical Apparatus: Hypertrophy and Recovery

Part 3.1: The 4-Day Hypertrophy Program

A 4-Day Upper/Lower Split to maximize muscle growth (hypertrophy) by training each muscle group twice per week. This split optimizes frequency, manages fatigue, and is time-efficient.

Day 1: Upper Body 1 (Push Focus)

  • Barbell Bench Press: 4 sets of 8-10 reps
  • Incline Dumbbell Press: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
  • Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
  • Lateral Raises: 3 sets of 12-15 reps
  • Triceps Pushdowns: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
  • Dumbbell Skullcrushers: 3 sets of 10-12 reps

Day 2: Lower Body 1 (Quad Focus)

  • Barbell Back Squats: 4 sets of 8-10 reps
  • Leg Press: 3 sets of 10-15 reps
  • Walking Lunges: 3 sets of 10-12 reps/leg
  • Leg Extensions: 3 sets of 12-15 reps
  • Standing Calf Raises: 4 sets of 15-20 reps

Day 4: Upper Body 2 (Pull Focus)

  • Pull-Ups (or Lat Pulldowns): 4 sets of 8-12 reps
  • Barbell Rows: 3 sets of 8-10 reps
  • Seated Cable Rows: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
  • Face Pulls: 3 sets of 15-20 reps
  • Barbell Curls: 3 sets of 10-12 reps
  • Hammer Curls: 3 sets of 10-12 reps

Day 6: Lower Body 2 (Hamstring/Glute Focus)

  • Romanian Deadlifts: 4 sets of 8-10 reps
  • Bulgarian Split Squats: 3 sets of 10-12 reps/leg
  • Lying Leg Curls: 3 sets of 12-15 reps
  • Glute Bridges/Hip Thrusts: 3 sets of 10-15 reps
  • Seated Calf Raises: 4 sets of 15-20 reps

The Fueling Strategy: High-Performance Nutrition

Supplementation Protocol: Timing for Efficacy

  • Creatine Monohydrate (5g): Consume post-workout on training days for enhanced uptake. On rest days, take with any meal to maintain saturation.
  • Whey Protein (20-25g): Take immediately post-workout to kickstart muscle protein synthesis when muscles are most receptive.
  • Milk: A powerful recovery drink. Consume post-workout for its mix of whey, casein, and carbs. Also drink pre-sleep for its slow-digesting casein, which mitigates muscle breakdown overnight.

Strategic Directives: Mindset & Sustainability

Sleep: A Non-Negotiable Performance Multiplier

With a 5:00 AM wake-up, 7-8 hours of quality sleep is a biological necessity. A consistent bedtime of 11:30 PM to 12:00 AM must be rigorously protected. Sleep is the foundation upon which all cognitive and physical gains are built.
